About Brgy360

Bridging technology and governance to empower residents and barangay administrations.

Purpose

To make barangay services more accessible, organized, and convenient for residents while helping barangay personnel manage requests and records efficiently.

Resident Access

Residents create an account, get verified, and request services online, reducing repeated trips to the barangay hall.

Digital Service Requests

Every service has its own request form. The resident provides information once; the system auto-fills the official document.

Barangay Personnel Management

Admins review, approve, generate, preview, and print documents, streamlining the entire processing workflow.

Transparency

Public budget and project information keeps the community informed and accountable.

Community Participation

Residents can report concerns and propose community projects, fostering active civic engagement.

Where do barangay funds go?

Barangay budget allocations are strictly administered in compliance with Republic Act No. 7160 (The Local Government Code) and DILG Full Disclosure policies to ensure fiscal transparency and community empowerment:

  • Infrastructure: Road repairs, drainage and flood mitigation canal desiltation, smart solar streetlights, and public assembly facilities.
  • Health: Barangay health station maintenance, maternal & infant diagnostic tools, medical missions, medicine procurement, and sanitation campaigns.
  • Administration: Daily barangay governance, digital portal maintenance, civil registries, document verification, and staff capacity development.
  • Social Services: Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) youth programs, Senior Citizens & PWD welfare initiatives, day care learning kits, and disaster relief reserves.

COMMUNITY ISSUES & CITIZEN DESK

Report a Concern

A transparent and accountable system to report neighborhood problems and track barangay action.

About this service

Reporting a concern lets you formally inform the barangay about a problem in your area — a damaged streetlight, uncollected garbage, a drainage blockage, excessive noise, or any matter that needs the attention of the barangay. Every concern is logged, given its own tracking number, and acted on by the assigned barangay personnel.

Filing a concern through this portal is available to registered residents so the barangay can verify the report and get back to you with the action taken.

How to send a concern

  1. Step 1

    Create a resident account or sign in to the portal.

  2. Step 2

    Open Report a Concern and choose a category — infrastructure, sanitation, safety, noise, public utility, or other.

  3. Step 3

    Describe the issue and give the exact location or nearest landmark.

  4. Step 4

    Submit the concern and keep the tracking number that is issued to you.

How the barangay responds

New

Your concern is logged and given a tracking number. Barangay personnel are notified.

In Review

The report is validated, the site is inspected, and it is assigned to the proper committee or office.

Resolved

The action taken is recorded and the resolution notes are attached to your concern for you to read.

Ready to report a concern?

Sign in to your resident account to file a concern and track the barangay's response, or create an account if you are not registered yet.
