Propose a Community Project

Submit grassroots project ideas directly to the Barangay Development Council (BDC) for inclusion in the Annual Barangay Development Plan.

How Citizen Project Proposals Work

Every year, 20% of Barangay Pallocan West's Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA) is earmarked for Community Development Fund projects. Verified residents can propose small-scale infrastructure, sanitation improvements, solar lighting, youth sports hubs, and greening initiatives. Proposals are reviewed by the BDC during the Barangay Assembly.
